⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots) is the only trophy flagged as possibly missable. One meditation spot is at Jason’s house in Chapter 1: after the mission Down to Earth, look for Lucia there in the morning, follow her to the balcony and meditate before you leave Chapter 1. Every other trophy is unmissable in a single playthrough with free roam after the story. Read its full guide →

Roadmap to the platinum

Five phases, in order. Finish the story first, meditate early in Chapter 1, then clean up in free roam. The 100% Completion checklist unlocks after the story at Touch Pad > Progress > 100% Completion.
